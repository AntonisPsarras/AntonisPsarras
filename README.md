Antonis (Anthony) Psarras

Greek high-school student in Thessaloniki, building software and hardware, aiming at physics and engineering at university.

Outside school, I am driven by turning an idea into something useful, from the first design to a working product. I started with a 3D printer and product design, then learned Python, PCB design, and soldering so I could connect software to microcontrollers and sensors rather than starting from Arduino boards. I have awards in national-level mathematics and physics competitions, and I study theoretical physics — especially quantum computing — which I hope to pursue at university.

ScholiLink — A Flutter student platform with Firebase auth, server-side Gemini, and Greek-first tools for schedule, homework, grades, and classroom chat. [GitHub](https://github.com/AntonisPsarras/Scholilink)

Aether Gravity — A real-time N-body space simulator in React, TypeScript, and Three.js, with GPU particles, Kerr-type black holes, and a universe sandbox. [GitHub](https://github.com/AntonisPsarras/Aether-Gravity) · [Live](https://aether-gravity.netlify.app/)

Guardian System — A household RFID entry and presence build: two printed ESP32 readers, Home Assistant, ESPHome, and camera-driven alerts. [GitHub](https://github.com/AntonisPsarras/guardian-system)

Wind Turbine System — A 3D-printed balcony turbine with yaw tracking, Schottky rectification, and TP4056 charging for an 18650 cell. [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2967248-wind-turbine-project-functional-simple-turbine#profileId-3327133)
